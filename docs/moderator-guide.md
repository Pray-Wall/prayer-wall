# Moderator guide

One page. Read it once before your first shift, and again whenever it changes.

> **Status: draft.** The crisis section must be approved by someone with crisis-line experience
> before launch. Their name or role and the date go in `docs/decisions.md`.

## Your job in one sentence

Judge **conduct against the seven rules, never belief**, and **never contact or reply to an author**.

## The seven rules

1. This wall is for prayer to God. Ask, give thanks, or pray for others.
2. No contact details, links, usernames or addresses.
3. No requests for money, gifts or business.
4. When praying for someone else, use a first name only and nothing that identifies them.
5. No preaching, arguing, or speaking against any person, group or faith.
6. No prayers for harm to anyone.
7. Keep it suitable for all ages.

Requests must be in English for now. Different traditions, words for God and styles of prayer are all welcome.

| Usually fine | Remove, with this reason |
| --- | --- |
| "Pray for my mother Ana, she has surgery on Friday." | "Pray for Ana Silva at St Mary's Hospital": *Identifies another person* |
| "Help me find work; we are behind on rent." | "Please send anything to my account": *Asks for money* |
| "Thank you, Lord, for healing my son." | "Only our church has the truth; pray the others wake up": *Preaching, arguing, or against others* |
| "I am angry at my brother. Pray I can forgive him." | "Pray that my brother gets what he deserves": *A prayer for harm* |
| "Please pray, my marriage is falling apart." | A sexual detail, or violence described graphically: *Not suitable for all ages* |

When unsure, press **Ask for a second opinion**. When it is hard for you personally, press **Skip**; someone else will take it, and nothing is recorded.

## The queue

Crisis first, then reported, then oldest. Each card shows the text, who it is for, the classifier's
guess, why it is waiting, reports, how long it has waited, and how many other requests the same
visitor had held or removed today. You never see who posted.

| Item | Aim | At the latest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crisis | 2 hours | 6 hours |
| Reported | 6 hours | 24 hours |
| Ordinary | 12 hours | 24 hours |

The classifier's guess is a hint, not a decision. It is often over-cautious; publish when the rules are met.

## Crisis requests (awaiting approval)

Someone may be thinking about ending their life, being hurt, or afraid for another person. These
are **never** deleted unread. The author has already been shown a support message with a link to
a helpline. You cannot reply, and must not try to contact them.

- **Publish as written**, unless it describes a method or means, a specific plan with a time or place, or details that identify a third person at risk.
- If it has no red Crisis tag, press **Mark as crisis** first.
- If you cannot publish it, **remove it with any reason**. The author does not see a rule; they see: "We could not show this request on the wall as it was written, but a person has read it and you are not forgotten." The support panel stays.
- Marking a request as crisis shows the support panel on the author's private page.
- If it upsets you, step away and tell the maintainer. You do not have to carry it alone either.

## When to call the maintainer at once

| Situation | You | Maintainer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A threat to another person, anything sexualising or endangering a child, or personal details posted to harm someone | Remove at once and note the request ID | Within 1 hour; follows the legal advice on reporting |
| A legal notice or a message from an authority | Do nothing and forward it | Answers through the contact address |
| A flood of posts or reports | Keep moderating; the breakers act by themselves | Decides when to return to normal |

Reach the maintainer at the contact address in `SECURITY.md` or the channel you were given when you joined. If they cannot be reached for 24 hours, the deputy named in `GOVERNANCE.md` acts.

## Never

- Reply to, contact or try to identify an author.
- Copy request text anywhere outside the queue, including screenshots.
- Decide by whether you share the author's faith.
