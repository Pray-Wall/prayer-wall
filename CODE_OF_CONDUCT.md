# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies in full to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions.

## In short

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- People of every tradition, and of none, contribute here. Do not preach, argue about belief,
  or speak against any person, group or faith.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or publishing of anyone's private information.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and give it gently.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **doublestrike.rar@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer
and handled confidentially. If the report concerns the maintainer, write to the deputy named in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may
warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
